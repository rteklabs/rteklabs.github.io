Privacy

Property Spot Map is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. The map builder stores a draft in your browser's local storage. It does not send your draft to a Property Spot Map server or create an account.

When you create a share link, your map title, client/reference text, property details, notes, categories, and manually added locations are encoded in the URL. Google places are represented by place IDs and their current details are fetched when the map opens. A share link is not encrypted: anyone with the link can view the included information. Avoid putting private client details in a map or share link.

Google Maps and Places load in your browser and receive map and search requests. Their use is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and Google Maps terms. GitHub Pages hosting is subject to GitHub's privacy statement.

You can remove the saved draft by using New map in the builder or clearing this site's browser storage. Shared links cannot be recalled from another person's browser after you send them.

Questions about this tool can be directed to rteklabs on GitHub.
