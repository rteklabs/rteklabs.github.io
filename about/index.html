What we build

We focus on useful utilities rather than bloated platforms: tools that are easy to understand, quick to use, and designed around a specific real-world problem.

Current public project

LAN Send is our current public release: a local-network transfer app for Android and Windows that sends files, text and clipboard content directly between paired devices.

How we think about software

Useful software should explain itself, stay out of the way, and give users clear control. We prefer straightforward workflows, practical defaults, and avoiding unnecessary cloud dependence where a local solution works better.
